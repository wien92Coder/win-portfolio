# Win Winarno Portfolio

Single-page bilingual (Bahasa/English) portfolio for Win Winarno, positioned as a Hospitality & Healthcare **Tech-Operator**. This glossary defines the content domain shared by the copy, the design system, and the i18n locale files.

## Positioning

**Tech-Operator**:
Win's positioning — a senior field operator (30+ years in hospitality & healthcare) who uses code as an operational tool, not a developer who happens to work in hospitality.
_Avoid_: developer, programmer, coder

**Vibe Coding**:
AI-assisted development (Claude Code, n8n, Notion, & AI) treated as an operational tool — the current "most effective tool" in the career narrative.
_Avoid_: prompt engineering (as a label for the practice)

## Site structure

**Proof Strip**:
The hero stat row — large display-font numbers with small mono-font labels showing source-specific achievement figures.
_Avoid_: stat bar, metrics row, KPI strip

**Food Cost Calculator**:
The fully functional widget (section 2) that computes COGS, actual food-cost ratio, gross profit, and recommended selling price from ingredient inputs; the site's core proof-of-work.
_Avoid_: calculator demo, widget (alone)

**Dual Competency Grid**:
The two-column section contrasting Operational & People Leadership with Vibe Coding & Tech Stack.
_Avoid_: skills grid, competencies table

## Case-study content

**SMART Care**:
Win's service-excellence training framework, cascaded via train-the-trainer; the shared methodology across the three hospital case studies.
_Avoid_: training program (generic)

**Case-study pillars**:
The two groupings of case studies — Pillar A, Healthcare Service Culture Transformation (SMC Tlogorejo, RSUD Rembang, Santa Elisabeth); Pillar B, Hospitality & Business Operations (Hanania Kitchen and Brew, Patuno Resort Wakatobi).
_Avoid_: sections, projects, testimonials
